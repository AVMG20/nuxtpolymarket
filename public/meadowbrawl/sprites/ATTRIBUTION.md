# Meadowbrawl sprite sheets

Pre-rendered from the following packs by Kay Lousberg (https://kaylousberg.com),
all released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0):

- KayKit Adventurers Character Pack 2.0 (free tier) — player characters and weapons
- KayKit Skeletons Character Pack 1.1 (free tier) — enemies and their weapons
- KayKit Character Animations 1.1 — the shared Rig_Medium clip library
- KayKit Fantasy Weapons Bits 1.0 (free tier) — spear, hammer and halberd

The sheets are baked by `scripts/meadowbrawl-sprites/bake.ts`; see its README.
