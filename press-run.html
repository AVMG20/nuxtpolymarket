Battler bench · matchup lab

Simulates the auto-battler's current rules: damage = energy cost × tier rate (₱3→1, ₱4→1.5, ₱5→1.75, ₱6→2, ₱8→2.5, ₱10→3 per ⚡), merge multipliers ×2.6 (3 copies) / ×5.5 (6 copies), active-as-shield combat with bounty prizes, round cap 30. Unit stat profiles are fitted against the live catalog (2026-08) — the shipped source of truth is shared/utils/battler/. Units are sampled independently from the tier profile, so same-card comparisons (merge value) read a few points lower here than a sim over the real catalog, where both sides share one card's exact stats.

Battler bench · draft weight
