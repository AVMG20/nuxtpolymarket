title: Hero Quest fixes
description: The Hero Quest welcome-back card stays put, and a run waiting at a boss earns at its full farming rate.

- **Hero Quest**: the welcome-back card no longer goes missing after time away; it waits until you press Continue.
- **Hero Quest**: a run waiting at a boss now earns Gold and XP at the full rate of the stage it farms, about ten times what it paid before.
