---
title: Hero Quest fixes
description: Hero Quest levels up twice as fast and opens its menus sooner, and a run waiting at a boss earns at its full farming rate.
date: 2026-10-13
---

- **Hero Quest**: the welcome-back card no longer goes missing after time away; it waits until you press Continue.
- **Hero Quest**: a run waiting at a boss now earns Gold and XP at the full rate of the stage it farms, about ten times what it paid before.
- **Hero Quest**: XP per kill is doubled, and the Shop, Battle Speed and Calendar are open from the start, with Loadouts, the Passive Tree, Raids, Traits and the Arena each opening a World sooner.
- **Hero Quest**: the game screen now fits your window's height too, with a Large, Mid or Small screen size in Settings.
