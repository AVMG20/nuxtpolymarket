---
title: Hackerz arrives
description: A new 3D lane defence, Hackerz, joins the active games.
date: 2026-10-13
---

- **Hackerz**: a new game. Hold a neon fence against waves of hackers in 3D, with its own Mainframe and best wave.
- **Hackerz**: kill chains, radar ping, cloaked phantoms, boss tricks, skippable double-credit bonus rounds and Z/X/C deployables.
- **Hackerz**: hostiles ride the glowing data lines, faster, and bleed in your blue ground; waves build to a final push.
- **Hackerz**: seven green hackers are always on the field while a path node can still be taken, splitting across two nodes, zapping between lines to dodge your shots and hacking with live terminals, and every node they take turns more ground red: a fast lane that shields and heals them, with islands for nodes taken out of turn.
- **Hackerz**: once every node falls the gate itself is disputed, unless you have the Grid Trap; every bonus round starts there, locked, and Packet Loss now freezes the whole board and clock in lag spikes while hostiles keep riding the lines (your turrets suffer it too).
- **Hackerz**: the DDoS final push now surges to match your firepower, and turrets cover their own lane first, going for the toughest hostile and leaving hackers for last.
- **Hackerz**: three hacked nodes close together put the lights out (ping to see), and call up a shield wall that guards their front while the wounded heal behind it.
- **Hackerz**: only armoured hostiles walk into your blue ground; the wounded fall back to heal in red while others cover them, and sappers run faster.
- **Hackerz**: later waves mix in far more fast runners (sappers, wisps, phantoms, gunships) among the armour.
- **Hackerz**: Packet Loss sends twice the light hostiles, scaled to your firepower; they surge after each lag spike, heavies charge straight, and nobody hangs back in bonus rounds.
- **Hackerz**: logic bombs wire onto nodes you hold or contest, ignore hackers and blow when the node falls; each hacked node posts its weakest nearby unit in front as a guard.
- **Hackerz**: from wave 15 the hackers come in two packs of nine, the second four seconds in.
- **Hackerz**: from wave 10 hackers bring bodyguards that shield them from your fire (two each from wave 20), and fallen guards are replaced.
- **Hackerz**: every turret mount you buy now shows up: a raised second row of four stands behind the first, for twelve in all.
- **Hackerz**: shots now stop on a hacked node and send a blue pulse down its lines, running further through your ground than theirs.
- **Hackerz**: a live syslog terminal in the HUD narrates the fight (captured and reclaimed nodes, quarantined viruses, crashed monitors) and carries the big warnings.
- **Hackerz**: sappers swarm the fence while the first nodes are fought over, up to four hackers team up on the first node, and the rest push ahead with a hostage as a shield.
- **Hackerz**: the portal now has several lines into the board and ripples where each hostile comes through.
- **Hackerz**: P or Esc pauses.
- **Hero Quest**: the welcome-back card no longer goes missing after time away; it waits until you press Continue.
