---
title: Hero Quest classes and prestige pacing
description: Stronger classes, faster later prestiges, a harder-hitting Raise Dead and a batch of fixes.
date: 2026-10-20
---

- **Hero Quest Classes**: every class now makes your Hero stronger than the one it was taken from, and the Witch Doctor's Raise Dead strikes every enemy.
- **Hero Quest**: levelling is slower, and later prestiges follow a steady schedule instead of taking many times longer than the one before.
- **Hero Quest Traits**: Auto Roll keeps 100 Trait Gems, so you can always load a saved board after a bad run.
- **Hero Quest Collections**: step to the previous or next entry from the detail screen with the arrows, the arrow keys or a swipe.
- **Hero Quest**: Treasure Hunter's Gambit and King's Ransom now grant a few seconds of income per cast instead of a few minutes.
- **Hero Quest**: prestiges pay whole Void Shards, and reloading during an Arena fight no longer shows a server error.
