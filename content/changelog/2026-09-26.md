## Neighcasso Derby

- You can always take a gate now, even with an empty wallet. Nothing is taken until the gates close, so the bet is what checks you can pay.
- A gate that can't be taken shows one clear error instead of three.

## Polytown

- **Buildings show their level**: every building changes shape and grows taller at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20, houses wear fixed colours per level, and every other building has its own colours so you can tell a bakery from a smithy.
