title: Polytown at night
description: Polytown gets a night mode with a sunset, a moonlit sky and a light on in every building and car.
date: 2026-11-10

- **Polytown**: the sun sets when your device switches to dark mode, and every building and car turns its lights on. The new sun/moon button can also follow the real sun in your time zone, or pin it to day or night.
- **Nitro Rush**: the Spectre joins the Garage, a low, wide prototype with a canopy cockpit, a shark fin and a blade of light round the tail.
