---
title: Polytown at night, Nitro Rush jumps
description: Polytown gets a night mode, and the Nitro Rush track builder gets road widths and jumps built from their own ramps.
date: 2026-11-10
---

- **Polytown**: the sun sets when your device switches to dark mode, and every building and car turns its lights on. The new sun/moon button can also follow the real sun in your time zone, or pin it to day or night.
- **Nitro Rush**: the Spectre joins the Garage, a low, wide prototype with a canopy cockpit, a shark fin and a blade of light round the tail.
- **Nitro Rush**: the track builder gets road widths, from narrow to very wide.
- **Nitro Rush**: build jumps from a ramp, a gap of any length and a landing; fly high enough to clear the walls.
- **Nitro Rush**: checkpoints only count in order.
