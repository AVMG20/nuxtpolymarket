---
title: Void Runner Wanted Levels
description: Void Runner gets a wanted level, an open sector and a pass over sound, gear and balance.
date: 2026-09-18
---

- **Wanted level**: killing raises the sector's heat, shown as five stars on the HUD. Mine quietly and it bleeds away; hunt for long enough and heavy wings come looking for you. Past four stars they arrive faster than you can lose them, so bank your hold or die out there.
- **Open sector**: the map no longer pushes you back towards the middle. Fly as far out as you like, and know that patrols out past the charted edge come harder.
- **Room to breathe**: the difficulty no longer builds from the five-minute mark, so there is time to reach the warden and fight it. Waves also stop piling in on top of a boss fight.
- **Shipyard in the hangar**: pick your next hull from a side panel while your ship stays on the pad, with bars comparing it against the one you fly.
- **Sound**: capital ships now go up with a bang, crits crack, shields break with their own sound, and rare drops ring out the moment they fall. Distant fights sit behind you instead of on top of you, and the combat music no longer stutters after a quiet stretch.
- **Gear balance**: the Lance Beam, Plasma Thrower, Scattergun and Siege Mortar all hit harder, the Railgun less. Crit rolls and the Prism Lens are worth taking, supplies cost a fraction of what they did, and the Wasp finally carries more than the starter hull.
- **Secondaries**: your missile rack rebuilds a warhead every few seconds and refills on every jump, and warheads do more to capital ships.
- **Turrets stay on target**: your turrets no longer wander off to chew on asteroids, and never open fire on a Coalition patrol just because your crosshair drifted over one. They shoot rock only while you are shooting it yourself.
- **Clearer screens**: the helm card shows five keys instead of eighteen, the shipyard explains what a hull costs you in each stat, and the workshop says what a rarity roll actually buys.
