---

- **Xeno**: a clean new look in dark and light, with a seed bar on top of the garden and every page using the full width.
- **Xeno plants**: brand-new drawings that grow from seedling to full bloom.
- **Xeno Nursery**: fill the left or right side of every idle pod with one seed.
- **Xeno market**: type your own keep amount and sell from a toolbar above your stacks.
- **Xeno artifacts**: pick how many to craft, and owned artifacts are grouped by type.
- **Colony**: a calmer look, typed amounts for hatching and selling, Fill for free slots and Collect all for builders.
- **Spreadsheet mode**: press `` ` `` (or pick it from your account menu) and Polynux turns into a spreadsheet; press it again to come back.
- **In the spreadsheet**: play Dice, Limbo, Wheel, the slots, Xeno and Colony, use the Bank and the Gem Exchange, run Hack Ops, start Polytown's recommended upgrades, and look up every other game.
- **Dead Front**: smoother zombies and teammates on a laggy connection, and lag spikes no longer undo your kills or buys.
- **Dead Front weapons**: five new box guns (Webley, Coach Gun, Winchester 1895, Chauchat and a flamethrower), a slide, and punchier recoil and gun movement.
- **Dead Front rarity**: guns come in Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary, with better rolls from the box in later rounds and upgrade kits as a rare prize.
- **Dead Front salvage**: kills drop scrap only you can pick up; spend it at a workbench on a sentry, a gramophone decoy, a field kit, a grenade launcher, a rarity upgrade or a call-in: artillery, a Zeppelin raid or a bomber that wipes out an area.
- **Dead Front supply crates**: zombies now and then drop a crate with a workbench item in it, yours for 15 seconds and then anyone's; plus a round challenge for extra salvage.
- **Dead Front fixes**: everyone starts with the M1911, the X-Ray's blasts keep their Pack-a-Punch colour, and the wunderweapon hits zombies up close.
