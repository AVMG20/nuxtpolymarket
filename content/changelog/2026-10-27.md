---
title: A new look for Xeno
description: Xeno gets a clean redesign with hand-drawn plants that grow in stages.
date: 2026-10-27
---

- **Xeno**: a clean new look in dark and light, with a seed bar on top of the garden and every page using the full width.
- **Xeno plants**: brand-new drawings that grow from seedling to full bloom.
- **Xeno Nursery**: fill the left or right side of every idle pod with one seed.
- **Xeno market**: type your own keep amount and sell from a toolbar above your stacks.
- **Xeno artifacts**: pick how many to craft, and owned artifacts are grouped by type.
- **Colony**: a calmer look, typed amounts for hatching and selling, Fill for free slots and Collect all for builders.
