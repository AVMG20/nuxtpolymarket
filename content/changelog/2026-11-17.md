---
title: Bigger Nitro Rush fields
description: Grow a Nitro Rush field into a bigger one with a tap, and edit the whole thing at once.
date: 2026-11-17
---

- **Nitro Rush fields**: tap + to grow a field, edit it all at once, and run roads in or out anywhere along an edge.
- **Nitro Rush checkpoints**: gates glow cyan and stand out on fields, and field blocks carry hazard stripes.
- **Nitro Rush maps**: joined fields show as one field on the minimap, and track cards show fields too.
- **Nitro Rush field obstacles**: pillars are now big towers, and blocks, pillars and walls carry ads.
- **Nitro Rush field lines**: put the start and finish on a field, and move where the road enters it.
