title: Your Nitro Rush tracks in Community
description: Your published Nitro Rush tracks show in the Community tab, and you can vote on them.
date: 2026-11-17
---

- **Nitro Rush community**: your published tracks show in the Community tab next to everyone else's, and you can vote on them too.
- **Nitro Rush track builder**: keep up to 7 tracks of your own, up from 5.
- **Dead Front**: a new comic look with cel shading, ink outlines, a painted night sky, hand-drawn walls and ground, new props and a comic-book HUD.
- **Dead Front weapons**: every gun is redrawn in the comic style, shotguns boom and blow zombies apart, and Pack-a-Punch adds perks like one-motion reloads, fire and chain lightning.
- **Dead Front rarity and salvage**: scrap drops far more often and upgrades are cheaper, and rarer guns glow, handle better and get an upgrade fanfare.
- **Dead Front**: the Iron Warlord storms in on milestone rounds, and zombies have audible footsteps.
- **Dead Front map**: a field hospital, a shelled bell tower, a windmill, craters and a skyline beyond the walls.
- **Dead Front effects**: inked smoke and blast stars, BOOM/POP words, and glowing lamps.
- **Dead Front workbench**: closes with a tap of F, Esc, right-click or by walking off.
- **Dead Front armory**: weapon cards show a level bar that fills up as the weapon levels to max.
- **Dead Front**: fixed half the screen turning black on some Windows PCs.
- **Dead Front**: lag no longer piles up into seconds of ping on a slow connection.
- **Riftborn** and **Rail Tycoon** have been retired.
