---
title: Command Marks on Short Runs
description: Boss kills and jumps now count on runs of any normal length.
date: 2026-09-21
---

- **Command Marks on short runs**: Void Runner no longer needs a 4 to 7 minute run before a capital ship kill counts. Any extraction longer than ten seconds pays its Marks, warp cores, relic and gear caches in full.
- **Jumps count right away**: jump depth is no longer held back by how long the run has lasted, so the depth 3 Mark and deeper loot bonus apply as soon as you get there.
- **Command Marks by sector**: the daily cap of 8 Marks is gone. Instead, harder sectors pay more: a run pays a quarter of its Marks in Halcyon Drift and up to double in The Abyss (28 at most). Pilot upgrades cost three times as many Marks, and the perks page shows the Marks you earned today.
- **No kill or cargo minimum**: Marks, blueprints and gear caches no longer need 10 kills and 100 cargo on the run.
- **Void Runner on PC monitors**: lasers, beacon rings and station lights keep their colour on Windows and Linux instead of turning into white glare, and the game renders sharp on big screens. New Anti-aliasing, Brightness and Glow settings in the pause menu replace the Low graphics toggle.
- **Two new Void Runner ships**: the **Tempest**, a sector 5 Stormrunner that is the fastest hull in the game and carries a new Slipstream ability, and the **Sovereign**, an obsidian, gold and sapphire flagship with a glittering aura that the yard only builds for pilots who already own every other ship.
