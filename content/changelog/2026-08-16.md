---
title: Call of Xeno Launch — Economy Rebalance for Run Games, Xeno & Colony
description: A new round-based survival shooter with permanent meta-upgrades and difficulty tiers, plus FIREWALL, Pathwarden and SHAPEZZ payouts retuned to a shared per-run band, and Xeno and Colony payouts and costs cut by 45-55%.
date: 2026-08-16
---

- **Call of Xeno — a new run-based shooter** — survive escalating rounds of the xeno horde, spending points inside the run on doors, weapons, perks, the mystery box and Pack-a-Punch. Points convert to coins when the run ends, and deeper rounds earn faster.
- **Four difficulty tiers** — Recruit, Veteran, Survivor and Nightmare. Each tier's horde is tougher, faster and more numerous, and pays 1× / 2.5× / 5× / 10×. The next tier unlocks at round 12, 16 or 25 on the one below, and best-round records are kept per tier.
- **Six permanent upgrade tracks** — War Chest, Body Armor, Adrenaline, Scavenger, Payout Contract and Sidearm, bought with coins and carried into every run: more starting points, more health, faster regen, cheaper in-run prices, a bigger end-of-run conversion, and better starting sidearms up to the AK-74.
- **Payouts the server can trust** — run start is stamped server-side, the wall clock caps how many points a run can be credited with, rounds settle at 100 at most, and the end-of-run settle is claim-guarded so a double request pays once. A two-hour cooldown separates runs and a leaderboard ranks best runs by tier then depth. The pause screen and death screen both show the exact capped amount the server will pay.
- **Payouts gated behind the Payout Contract** — a bare account tops out under 1M per run however deep it goes, and each contract level raises both the conversion and the ceiling. A maxed contract on Nightmare pays roughly 2M for a round-50 run to a player who farmed points well, and the absolute cap of 3M only arrives at round-100 pace: past round 50 the wall-clock ceiling flattens, so depth pays progressively less per round.
- **A shared payout band** — FIREWALL, Pathwarden, SHAPEZZ and Pirate Raid now aim at the same per-run economy: roughly 1k–50k on a bare account, 50k–250k once the first upgrades land, and 1–2M on a fully-invested one. Every upgrade tree now costs a few hundred million and takes about three hundred endgame runs to finish, instead of ranging from 373 million to 623 **billion**.
- **Cheaper upgrade trees** — these games were originally priced as coin sinks; prestige took that job, so the back halves of every tree have come down to reach. The FIREWALL Mainframe drops from 167B to 513M, the Pathwarden boost tree from 623B to 431M, and the SHAPEZZ workshop from 5.35B to 495M. Price curves are now a single exponential rather than doubling to level ten and then jumping by a factor of hundreds.
- **FIREWALL payouts retuned** — a bare wall could reach wave 19 on Probe and bank six figures for it, because coin value compounded hard with depth. Depth still pays, but the difficulty multiplier now decides what a run is worth: Probe pays a fraction of what Zero Day does, and the reward spread across the five settings is far wider than the spread in enemy health.
- **Pathwarden pays for the march, not for showing up** — checkpoint grants were a guaranteed 525k for a realm-1 clear that needed no upgrade at all. They are now a small floor, and the Aether you choose not to spend on defenses is the income. The cash-out rate climbs steeply with realm, realms hit noticeably harder, and Verdant Bounty moved from gems to coins so the coin tree finally raises what a march earns.
- **SHAPEZZ hits harder and banks more** — permanent workshop levels now scale to 7x damage and 6x health, and the payout ceiling follows the real earning curve instead of a quadratic that was clipping honest long runs by up to 3x. A maxed workshop on a clean run now lands in the 1–2M band.
- **Xeno Payouts Cut** — every plant sells for less: T1–T4 down 45%, T5/T6 down 50%, T7–T9 down 55%. Yesterday's rebalance fixed the *shape* of the tier curve but left the whole thing sitting far above every other idle on the site, which is how portfolios reached 600–700M before anyone had finished T7.
- **Colony Payouts Cut** — the same treatment for foraged items: T1/T2 down 45%, T3/T4 down 48%, T5/T6 down 53%. Xeno and Colony are still the two biggest earners on the site by a wide margin — they're just no longer a hundred times ahead of Miner and Hack Ops.
- **Costs Cut To Match** — grid and breeder slots, the three global Xeno upgrade ladders, bug spawn costs, colony track levels, habitat level-ups and feed prices all came down by the same factor. Nothing takes longer to afford than it did yesterday, and payback on a new bug or a new slot is unchanged — only the rate coins leave these two games moved.
- **Tier Curves Untouched** — the step between every tier and the next, and the balance between Xeno and Colony at equal days played, are both exactly where the last rebalance left them.
- **Balance scripts** — `bun run balance:firewall`, `balance:pathwarden`, `balance:shapezz` and `balance:pirates` print the investment-versus-difficulty payout grid for each game, so these curves stay checkable rather than asserted.
