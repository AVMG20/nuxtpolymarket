---
title: Void Runner, Rebuilt in 3D
description: Void Runner is now a third-person 3D space shooter.
date: 2026-09-17
---

- **New space game**: Void Runner is now a 3D space shooter. Fly through five sectors, mine asteroids, fight raiders, wardens and hidden capital ships, and dock to bank your haul.
- **Ships and gear**: build ten hulls, from the one-turret Sparrow to the Leviathan. Craft guns, turrets, armour and shields in five tiers, each with a random rarity, and level them up.
- **Skills and relics**: unlock pilot skills with small skill trees, and find rare relic mods that give your gear special effects.
- **Deeper runs**: jump through gates into tougher zones, fire secondary weapons and devices, scan for hidden caches and data logs, and earn Command Marks for permanent perks.
- **Bounties and salvaged gear**: every run rolls two bounties that pay a burst of salvage and pilot XP, and elites, wardens and vaults can drop salvaged gear that you open when you extract.
- **Gentler start**: a step-by-step pilot guide teaches one thing at a time, from flying forward to building your second ship, and the objectives panel now shows no more than three goals.
- **Clearer flight**: space dust no longer glares when you boost on high graphics, and you can play in fullscreen with F11 or the button in the hangar.
- **New hangar menus**: every menu now opens as a full-screen page with a short explanation, and the spinning ship only shows on the hangar screen. A new Loadout screen lays out your hardpoints and lets you click or drag gear into each slot, with a score comparison on every item.
- **Clearer workshop**: the workshop is split into My gear, Craft and Relic mods, with a step-by-step crafting flow and a note on what is stopping a craft.
- **Know your buttons**: the HUD names your device, the first use explains what it does, and scans tell you where to fly next. The pilot guide explains each key instead of just asking you to press it.
- **Easier on the eyes**: explosions and screen flashes are softer, a new Reduce flashes option in the pause menu dims them further, and a hangar button stops the camera circling your ship.
- **No silent losses**: the game asks before you close or reload mid-run, and tells you if a run was cut short.
