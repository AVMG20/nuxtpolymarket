---
title: Void Runner, Rebuilt in 3D
description: Void Runner is now a third-person 3D space shooter.
date: 2026-09-17
---

- **New space game**: Void Runner is now a 3D space shooter. Fly through five sectors, mine asteroids, fight raiders, wardens and hidden capital ships, and dock to bank your haul.
- **Ships and gear**: build ten hulls, from the one-turret Sparrow to the Leviathan. Craft guns, turrets, armour and shields in five tiers, each with a random rarity, and level them up.
- **Skills and relics**: unlock pilot skills with small skill trees, and find rare relic mods that give your gear special effects.
