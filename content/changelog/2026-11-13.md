---
title: Nitro Rush trackside
description: Nitro Rush courses get chevrons in the bends and ads for every Polynux game.
date: 2026-11-13
---

- **Nitro Rush**: chevrons on the outside of every bend show how sharp it is, and ads for every Polynux game now line the walls, hang over the straights, fly as flags and stand on rooftops, without clipping into roads above.
- **Nitro Rush jumps**: yellow arrows and a hazard stripe on the road, plus a board on each wall, warn you of every jump.
- **Nitro Rush medals**: tracks whose jumps need a boost pad's full speed now get medal times.
