---
title: Dead Front goes comic
description: Dead Front trades its voxel look for cel shading, ink outlines and a hand-drawn comic style.
date: 2026-11-13
---

- **Dead Front**: cel shading and ink outlines, with a painted night sky, an inked moon and a burning skyline.
- **Dead Front**: walls, roofs and ground are hand-drawn bricks, cobbles, planks and tiles instead of blocks.
- **Dead Front**: new props, trees, graves, lamps and grass, and a comic-book HUD and menus.
