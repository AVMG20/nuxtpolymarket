---
title: Void Runner Capitals
description: Two new boss ships, a jump-only gunship and void jumps that tell you what you are flying into.
date: 2026-09-20
---

- **Tyrant Siege Cruiser**: a hammerhead battlecruiser now hides in every zone. It has no shield, but three of its guns are armoured into the hull and cannot be destroyed. An EMP knocks them out for a few seconds. At 66% hull it opens its rocket silos, and at 33% it calls in a Mauler and two Ravagers. Its stern furnace takes 50% more damage.
- **Eclipse Harbinger**: a ring ship with about three times the Dreadnought's hull waits in every zone past a jump gate. Break the four shield pylons on its ring to reach the hull. Its lance emitters never stop, two pylons reignite at half hull, and below 35% the core drags you in before it bursts. Burn away when it pulls.
- **Boss rewards**: both pay warp cores, relic caches, a gear cache and Command Marks. The Harbinger also drops a fuel cell.
- **Clearer jumps**: the jump gate shows each zone's upsides in green and downsides in red, plus how dangerous the jump is. The same chips show when you arrive and stay on the HUD.
- **Jumped zones look different**: each zone type has its own sky, fog, rocks and hazards.
- **Desolator**: a new gunship found only past a jump gate. Its void orbs curve in on you and its storm rocket fouls the space where it bursts.
- **Deeper is harder**: each jump raises hostile hull and damage more than before, while loot grows at the same rate.
- **New gear art**: every gun, turret, armour plate, shield, secondary, device and relic mod in Void Runner has a redrawn icon.
- **Clearer loadout**: gear you already have fitted is tagged with the slot it sits in, the item in the selected slot is outlined, and turret, armour and shield slots are numbered.
- **Polytown and Void Runner on the leaderboard**: both games now add to your upgrade score and show beside the other games. Polytown counts milestones claimed and research finished. Void Runner counts sectors cleared, pilot level, hulls owned, station system levels and trade contracts.
