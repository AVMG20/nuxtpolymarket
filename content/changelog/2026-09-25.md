## New Game: Neighcasso Derby

- **[Neighcasso Derby](/games/neighcasso)** is a multiplayer horse race where you draw the horses. Grab one of six starting gates, pick a horse from your stable, set any bet and hit Ready.
- The race starts once every horse in the gates is ready (at least two). When two are ready, the rest have 45 seconds before they get scratched. Players who arrive mid-race wait for the next one.
- Bets are matched: every horse races for the smallest bet in the gates, so every horse has the same odds. Bet 100k against a 10k player and only 10k of yours is on the line. Winner takes the pot.
- **Race solo against bots.** Click "add a bot" on any empty gate (or use the Bots −/+). Each bot matches the stake, so more bots mean a lower chance but a bigger pot: one bot is a coin flip for about 2×, five bots is 1 in 6 for about 6×.
- When bots run, the house keeps 1% of the pot, so every bet returns 99%. Races with only players have no fee.
- Expect lead changes, zoomies, naps, moonwalks and the occasional faceplant before the winner crosses the line.
- Draw with Body, Legs, Tail and Head pens: legs gallop, tails wag and heads bob. Any shape runs, horse or not.
- The bet slip beside the gates lists every horse with its player, stake, win chance, career win rate and your profit this session, plus the pot and what the winner gets.
- Save horses to your stable and race them again and again. Each horse keeps its win record.
- Share a horse with a link. Anyone who opens it can adopt a copy into their own stable.

## Hack Ops

- **Hack Ops double deploy fix**: pressing Deploy twice in quick succession, or deploying while an auto-deploy was collecting, could send the same squad out on several ops at once. Cancelling one of them left the agents stuck on the others, so the squad couldn't be deployed again. A squad now only ever goes out once.
