---
title: Hack Ops Deploy Fix
description: Deploying a squad twice by accident no longer sends it on two ops at once.
date: 2026-09-25
---

- **Hack Ops double deploy fix**: pressing Deploy twice in quick succession, or deploying while an auto-deploy was collecting, could send the same squad out on several ops at once. Cancelling one of them left the agents stuck on the others, so the squad couldn't be deployed again. A squad now only ever goes out once.
