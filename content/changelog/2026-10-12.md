title: Hero Quest and a forgiving Mayor streak

date: 2026-10-12
---

- **[Hero Quest](/hero-quest)**: a new idle auto-battler under Idle Games. Lead a party through ten worlds, pull Champions, Gear, Skills and Artifacts, and prestige for Void Shards.
- **Mayor streak**: missing a day no longer breaks the streak; it picks up where you left off, one day per day.
- **Hero Quest**: the Gacha and Collections now open the first time your party falls, not only at the World 1 boss, and the battle screen fills the page width.
- **Hero Quest**: every Gem purchase now asks for a second press, under one Gem Spending setting.
