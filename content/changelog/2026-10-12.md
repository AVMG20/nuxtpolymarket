---
title: Hero Quest and a forgiving Mayor streak
description: Hero Quest opens under Idle Games, and missing a day no longer breaks the Polytown Mayor streak.
date: 2026-10-12
---

- **[Hero Quest](/hero-quest)**: a new idle auto-battler under Idle Games. Lead a party through ten worlds, pull Champions, Gear, Skills and Artifacts, and prestige for Void Shards.
- **Mayor streak**: missing a day no longer breaks the streak; it picks up where you left off, one day per day.
- **Voxel Arena**: new Singularity, Hologram Decoy and Arc Overcharge abilities, six new boons and an Ion Surge wave event.
- **Voxel Arena enemies**: Splitters, laser Snipers, tunnelling Burrowers and an armoured Juggernaut boss that alternates with the Titan.
- **Voxel Arena**: waves pay about a third more credits, enemy models got more detail, and a crash when explosions chained together is fixed.
