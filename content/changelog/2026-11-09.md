---
title: Craft Max and the Nitro Rush Garage
description: Hero Quest's Collections get a Craft Max button, and Nitro Rush gets a Garage to pick your car in.
date: 2026-11-09
---

- **Hero Quest**: a Craft Max button next to Craft spends your Essence on as many copies as it buys, stopping once the item is maxed.
- **Nitro Rush**: pick your car and paint in the new Garage, including The OG, the game's first car, and the Roadster; every car drives the same and looks as big as it hits, and ghosts show the car they were driven in.
