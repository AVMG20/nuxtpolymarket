title: Craft Max and the Roadster
description: Hero Quest's Collections get a Craft Max button, and Nitro Rush gets a second car.
date: 2026-11-09
---

- **Hero Quest**: a Craft Max button next to Craft spends your Essence on as many copies as it buys, stopping once the item is maxed.
- **Nitro Rush**: pick the new Roadster in the garage next to the Kestrel.
