- **Hackerz**: a new game. Hold a neon fence against waves of hostiles in 3D, with its own Mainframe, turrets, deployables and best wave.
- **Hackerz**: hackers race to take the path nodes and turn the ground red; shoot a hacked node to take it back.
- **Hackerz**: radar ping, cloaked phantoms, shield walls, bodyguards, boss tricks and double-credit bonus rounds.
- **Hackerz**: every wave ends in a final push, and a live syslog narrates the fight. P or Esc pauses.
