---
title: Dead Front
description: A new co-op voxel zombies shooter set in a ruined 1917 village.
date: 2026-10-18
---

- **Dead Front**: new co-op round-based voxel zombies FPS for up to 4 players, with 1900s weapons, perks, a mystery box, three Pack-a-Punch tiers and a wonder weapon behind a guided easter egg.
- **Dead Front progression**: weapons you find unlock for your loadout, weapon levels unlock attachments, and perk research unlocks modifiers you can equip.
