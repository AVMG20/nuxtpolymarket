---
title: Polytown supply routes
description: See which workshops feed each other, and what the trip costs.
date: 2026-10-07
---

- **Polytown**: selecting a workshop lights up the road each delivery travels and marks every supplier feeding it. Hover a supplier to see how far the goods come and how much of the load survives the trip.
- **Polytown**: the resource bar is wider, so stock of 100K and more no longer runs into the hourly rate.
