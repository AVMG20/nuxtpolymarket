---
title: Xeno & Colony Economy Rebalance
description: Xeno plant values and upgrade costs raised across the board, Colony yield and speed tuned down, and Colony research reworked into a resource multiplier that applies to bugs you already own.
date: 2026-08-15
---

- **Xeno Payouts Raised** — every plant from T2 upward now sells for far more, with the biggest raises in the T3–T7 range that used to earn almost nothing. Income now climbs steadily 2.5–5x per tier instead of staying flat until T8 and then jumping 40x at once.
- **Xeno Upgrades Made Reachable** — Xenoflora Abundance no longer costs 801 billion to max; the whole ladder was repriced against income you actually have, and Temporal Cultivation's final level came down from 250 billion to 60 billion.
- **Colony Research Reworked** — each research level now multiplies everything that species forages by +25%, up to double at level 4. The multiplier applies colony-wide to bugs already in your terrarium, not just ones bought afterwards, and shows on each bug's tooltip. The wider speed and yield rolls are unchanged.
- **Colony Foraging Yield Tuned** — the habitat track now grants +1 yield level per level instead of +2, so it no longer drowns out every other upgrade you own.
- **Colony Speed Cap Lowered** — the colony-wide ceiling on stacked speed bonuses dropped from 85% to 75%, which affects late-game terrariums only and leaves early colonies untouched.
- **Hybrid Harvest Fixed** — the flat yield bonus from grid artifacts and global upgrades is now applied once per harvest instead of once per resource, which was paying multi-resource hybrids several times over.
- **Breeder Results Sealed** — a breed's outcome is no longer revealed before its timer finishes, closing a loophole where a run could be cancelled and rerolled for free once the result was known.
