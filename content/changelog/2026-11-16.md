title: Nitro Rush votes and popular tracks
description: Vote on every Nitro Rush track, sort by top voted or most raced, and unpublish without losing times.
date: 2026-11-16
---

- **Nitro Rush tracks**: unpublishing a track keeps its times and votes, and they come back when you publish the same road again.
- **Nitro Rush votes**: vote on the game's own tracks too, and sort the Tracks and Community tabs by top voted or most raced.
- **Nitro Rush fields**: build open fields with boost pads, gates, blocks and pillars; try one on The Open Field.
