---
title: Jewel Mines Replace the Miner
description: The Miner idle game is retired; Polytown now digs jewels that convert into gems.
date: 2026-09-12
---

- **Miner retired** — `/miner` and its subpages are gone (they redirect to Polytown), along with the Miner entries in the sidebar, search, AI assistant, prestige shop and leaderboard.
- **Jewel Mine** — a new tier-2 Polytown building that digs jewels at the usual workshop pace. It needs a lot of residents, and it is the only building capped at two per town.
- **Gems tab in the market** — convert jewels into gems (3,600 jewels per gem, about 16 gems a day from two maxed mines). Jewels stock, sell and trade like any other good, but the bulk-sell buttons skip them unless you tick the new checkbox.
- **Prospector goal** — 5 gems for building your first Jewel Mine.
