---
title: Nitro Rush, Rail Tycoon and a clearer Xeno
description: New games Nitro Rush, a neon time trial racer with a track builder, and Rail Tycoon, a multiplayer railway game in persistent worlds, and Xeno shows what you bred before you collect.
date: 2026-11-04
---

- **Nitro Rush**: a new neon time trial racer; drift to charge your nitro, chase the best lap on circuits and sprints, race ghosts and other players live, and build, publish and vote on community tracks.
- **Nitro Rush**: walls now cost real speed, so riding the outside wall through a corner is slower than braking for it.
- **Rail Tycoon**: a new multiplayer railway game; build lines between towns and industries with friends in persistent worlds.
- **Rail Tycoon**: make a world public to list it in the public lobby where anyone can join, and give each world a drawing.
- **Xeno Nursery**: finished pods show the plant, its speed and yield, and whether it mutated before you collect.
- **Xenopedia**: every species is named with its recipe, and cards show the best speed and yield you own.
- **Xeno artifacts**: the owned list shows how many you own, how many are fitted and the charges left.
- **Polytown**: the town loads much faster, you see every other mayor's town however far away it is, and the woods now stretch across the whole realm.
- **Polytown**: new towns are founded right next to everyone else, and towns that started far away have moved in.
