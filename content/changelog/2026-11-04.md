title: Rail Tycoon, a clearer Xeno and the Kestrel
description: New game Rail Tycoon, a multiplayer railway game in persistent worlds, and Xeno shows what you bred before you collect. A new race car for Nitro Rush.
date: 2026-11-04
---

- **Rail Tycoon**: a new multiplayer railway game; build lines between towns and industries with friends in persistent worlds.
- **Rail Tycoon**: make a world public to list it in the public lobby where anyone can join, and give each world a drawing.
- **Xeno Nursery**: finished pods show the plant, its speed and yield, and whether it mutated before you collect.
- **Xenopedia**: every species is named with its recipe, and cards show the best speed and yield you own.
- **Xeno artifacts**: the owned list shows how many you own, how many are fitted and the charges left.
- **Nitro Rush**: a new race car, the long-nosed Kestrel, with a full racing livery in whichever paint you pick.
