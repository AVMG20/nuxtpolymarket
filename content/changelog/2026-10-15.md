---
title: Hero Quest fixes
description: Raid Keys arrive at midnight, raid bosses use their specials, the Arena and Collections get fixes, the wiki explains every screen, and the economy is tightened.
date: 2026-10-15
---

- **Hero Quest**: in the Arena shop, Gems now cost 10 Medals, Seal raid Keys 150 and Trait raid Keys 90.
- **Hero Quest**: closed a few ways to collect rewards more than once, and a prestige now empties the Gold chest along with your balance.
- **Hero Quest**: daily raid Keys now arrive at midnight UTC for every raid and show without a reload, and calendar and milestone Keys stack on top of them.
- **Hero Quest**: every Trait Raid level climbed now pays noticeably more Trait Gems.
- **Hero Quest**: the Gilded Knight, the Deepcoil, God's Forge and Shardcaller Beast now use their special attacks.
- **Hero Quest**: every fallen Arena fighter now plays its death animation.
- **Hero Quest**: the Arena battle log shows only the last 24 hours and fits on one page.
- **Hero Quest**: your Arena defence now grows stronger with your account, and the Defence tab shows its Traits and explains what a defence does.
- **Hero Quest**: a new Descriptions setting switches Collections items between short lines and Detailed, which shows exactly what each does, including how much your banked Gold boosts wealth skills.
- **Hero Quest**: new menu items now appear once the boss fight has finished playing.
- **Leaderboard**: the Hero Quest collection now counts as a completion percentage.
- **Hero Quest**: numbers in the game and on the wiki are rounded to at most two decimals.
- **Hero Quest**: the wiki has a new Screens section with an article for every menu screen, explaining each button and symbol.
- **Hero Quest**: the wiki has a side menu, and its Screens and Content sections fold open while you're reading one of their pages.
- **Hero Quest**: the Arena win milestone now tops out at 300 Medals, and players on equal Rating are ranked by who got there first.
- **Prestige**: ascending now resets Hero Quest along with every other game.
- **Hackerz**: a Database of every threat, weapon, turret and defence, each with a 3D model and a live demo; entries decrypt as you meet or buy them.
- **Hackerz**: new hostile behaviours: memory-leaking Daemons, healing Wisps, a spider-legged ROOTKIT, BLACK ICE walls, a shattering ZERO DAY and shield-carrying Wardens.
- **Hackerz**: four new deployables: Portscan, Cosmic Blip, White Hat and Power Surge.
- **Hackerz**: the Longbore glides along a rail and punches through every body in line; the Tesla Coil's arcs now visibly chain.
- **Hackerz**: warheads glitch everything they catch; the ICE pulse washes your nearest lost nodes back to blue.
- **Hackerz**: easier opening waves, a new-look uplink and glowing Mainframe cards.
