title: Hero Quest fixes
description: Raid Keys arrive at midnight, raid bosses use their specials, the Arena and Collections get fixes, and the economy is tightened.
date: 2026-10-15

- **Hero Quest**: Gems in the Arena shop now cost 10 Medals each, up from 5.
- **Hero Quest**: closed a few ways to collect rewards more than once, and a prestige now empties the Gold chest along with your balance.
- **Hero Quest**: daily raid Keys now arrive at midnight UTC for every raid and show without a reload, and calendar and milestone Keys stack on top of them.
- **Hero Quest**: every Trait Raid level climbed now pays noticeably more Trait Gems.
- **Hero Quest**: the Gilded Knight, the Deepcoil, God's Forge and Shardcaller Beast now use their special attacks.
- **Hero Quest**: every fallen Arena fighter now plays its death animation.
- **Hero Quest**: the Arena battle log shows only the last 24 hours and fits on one page.
- **Hero Quest**: your Arena defence now grows stronger with your account, and the Defence tab shows its Traits and explains what a defence does.
- **Hero Quest**: every Champion, Skill, Gear piece and Artifact in Collections now shows exactly what it does, including how much your banked Gold boosts wealth skills.
- **Hero Quest**: new menu items now appear once the boss fight has finished playing.
- **Leaderboard**: the Hero Quest collection now counts as a completion percentage.
- **Hero Quest**: numbers in the game and on the wiki are rounded to at most two decimals.
