---
title: Call of Xeno Workbench and Wonder Weapon Rework
description: The Workshop gains a workbench that sells deployable sentry guns, escort drones and singularities, the Xeno Ray hits far harder, and the killstreak multiplier is gone in favour of plain Call of Duty points.
date: 2026-08-18
---

- **Workbench** — a new bench in the Workshop sells deployable equipment. Interacting opens an in-game menu the horde does not pause for: walk away or press F to close, Esc still pauses.
- **Sentry Gun (10,000)** — a tripod auto-turret that holds its ground for 60 seconds and tracks its own targets.
- **Escort Drone (12,500)** — follows you around the map and fires on the horde for 90 seconds, at a slower cadence than the sentry.
- **Singularity (15,000)** — an 8-second gravity well that drags every nearby hostile into its centre and grinds them down. Giants take little damage but are still pulled in, making it a reliable escape from a bad corner.
- **Equipment damage** — all workbench units deal a percentage of each enemy's maximum health, resisted differently per enemy type, so a sentry feels strong on round 5 and on round 50 alike.
- **Equipment HUD** — carried units show as small silhouettes next to the ammo counter with an E keycap; press E to deploy the first one in front of you.
- **Equipment drops** — kills have a 0.5% chance to leave a random unit on the floor. Pick it up like a power-up; a full pocket (three units) leaves it lying there, and it fades after 30 seconds.
- **Run checkpoints** — mid-run saves now carry undeployed equipment, so a crash no longer costs the sentry you were holding.
- **Xeno Ray** — base damage tripled from 620 to 1,860. The wonder weapon now punches like one.
- **Killstreaks removed** — the chained-kill point multiplier is gone. Points are flat Call of Duty rates again: 10 a hit, 100 a kill, 120 a headshot, 130 a knife kill, with Double Points and multi-kill bonuses unchanged.
