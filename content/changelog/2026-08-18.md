---
title: Call of Xeno Workbench
description: New deployable equipment from the Workshop workbench, a much stronger Xeno Ray, and killstreaks replaced by flat Call of Duty points.
date: 2026-08-18
---

- **Workbench** — new bench in the Workshop. Opens a menu without pausing the game; buy with points or number keys, F closes.
- **Sentry Gun (10k)** — auto-turret, holds its ground for 60s.
- **Escort Drone (12.5k)** — follows you and shoots for 90s.
- **Singularity (15k)** — 8s black hole that pulls everything in and grinds it down. Great panic button.
- All equipment deals % max-HP damage, useful on every round. Deploy with E; kills have a 0.5% chance to drop a free unit on the floor.
- **Xeno Ray** — damage tripled to 1,860.
- **Killstreaks removed** — points are flat CoD rates again (10 hit / 100 kill / 120 headshot).
