- **Nitro Rush**: every track has bronze, silver and gold medal times to beat.
- **Nitro Rush**: on circuits, crossing the start line fills your nitro back up.
- **Nitro Rush**: play with a controller, in the menus as well as on the road.
- **Nitro Rush**: boost pads in the track builder, across the whole road or just one half.
- **Nitro Rush**: wall hits no longer bounce you back or spin the car round, but any touch now costs most of your speed, so a clean lap is always the fastest.
- **Nitro Rush**: switching quickly from one drift to the other no longer sends you back into the first one.
- **Nitro Rush**: you can have up to 5 custom tracks, and My Tracks shows how many you have.
- **Nitro Rush**: corkscrews start and end on a bank leaning their way, and no longer dip into the ground.
- **Nitro Rush**: the builder's drawer tells you why a section can't go where you're building.
