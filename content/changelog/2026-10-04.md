---
title: Smoother building in Polytown
description: New build shortcuts, clearer placement and fresh sounds.
date: 2026-10-04
---

- **Polytown build controls**: right-click lets go of what you hold, Shift+R rotates back, C copies a building, U upgrades, 1–9 and Tab pick from the build bar.
- **Polytown placement**: the ghost glides between tiles, its door arrow stays visible, it turns red before you click when you're short, and new buildings land with a thud and new sounds.
- **Mayor streak**: open Polytown daily to unlock a 30-day reward track, with gem-filled chests on days 7, 14, 21 and 30 that burst open full screen.
- **Polytown boosts**: rare 1-hour Builder's rush and Production surge, plus chest-only Instant finish, Free builder, Market day and Lucky chest; rarer chests hold more of them.
- **Polytown contracts**: the town hall posts three daily orders for goods you make at twice the floor price, with bonus gems to claim for filling all three.
- **Polytown loads faster**: your own town shows up first and the neighbours rise in around it.
- **Welcome back**: a brighter summary of what your town made while you were away.
