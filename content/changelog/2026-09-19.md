---
title: Void Runner Refit
description: New ships, turrets and drones, a calmer hangar, and deep sectors that demand real gear.
date: 2026-09-19
---

- **New looks**: the Mule, Aegis, Hive, Bastion and Leviathan are all-new models, the smaller hulls gained detail, and every turret and drone was rebuilt.
- **Cleaner hangar**: Loadout, Workshop, Skills, Station, Market, Codex and Records were redesigned to be calmer and easier to read, with a Launch button always in the top bar.
- **Deep sectors bite back**: from sector 3 on hostiles hit far harder and wardens carry much more hull. Armour and shields give about two thirds more to match. Crafting stays cheap enough to repeat for a better roll; levelling T3 to T5 gear is where the cost went up.
- **Sector clears always count**: fast warden kills are no longer thrown out, a second tab can't end your run, and the launch bar says what opens the next sector.
- **Every model has its own price**: fancier guns, turrets and devices now cost a little more than the starter model and ask for a signature material (iridium for beams, xenite for railguns and mortars). The Workshop also shows each weapon's damage type.
