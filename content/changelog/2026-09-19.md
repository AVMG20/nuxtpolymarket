---
title: Void Runner Shipyard Refit
description: New capital hulls, sharper turrets and drones, and sector clears that always count.
date: 2026-09-19
---

- **New capital hulls**: the Mule, Aegis, Hive, Bastion and Leviathan are all-new models. The Mule hauls mismatched freight boxes over slung fuel tanks, the Aegis flies behind six kite shields and a projector ring, the Hive is a twin-hull carrier with a lit launch tunnel and honeycomb drone cells, the Bastion is a battleship with a triple main battery and armoured casemates, and the Leviathan carries its lance between two long prow tines.
- **Every hull detailed**: the Sparrow, Wasp, Kestrel, Phantom and Seraph keep their shapes and gain livery bands, flaps, ordnance under the wings, aerials and nose probes. The Wasp finally has its stripes.
- **New turrets and drones**: all eight turrets were rebuilt with their own character: ammo drums, shell racks, focusing claws, coil stacks, capacitor cells. Each wears its weapon colour as paint as well as light. Drones are now proper little twin-boom fighters with a chin gun.
- **Sector clears always count**: a fast warden kill is no longer thrown out for being too quick, and if the station ever does refuse one, the debrief says so. The debrief also tells you which sector your clear opened.
- **A second tab can't end your run**: opening Void Runner in another tab while you fly no longer closes the run underneath you, which used to cost the hold and the sector clear on extraction.
- **What opens the next sector**: the launch bar and the Wardens codex now say it outright: destroy the warden and dock in the same run.
