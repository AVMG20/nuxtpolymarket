---
title: Void Runner Refit
description: New ships, turrets and drones, a calmer hangar, and deep sectors that demand real gear.
date: 2026-09-19
---

- **New looks**: the Mule, Aegis, Hive, Bastion and Leviathan are all-new models, the smaller hulls gained detail, and every turret and drone was rebuilt.
- **Cleaner hangar**: Loadout, Workshop, Skills, Station, Market, Codex and Records were redesigned to be calmer and easier to read, with a Launch button always in the top bar.
- **Deep sectors bite back**: from sector 3 on hostiles hit far harder and wardens carry much more hull. Armour and shields give about two thirds more to match. Crafting stays cheap enough to repeat for a better roll; levelling T3 to T5 gear is where the cost went up.
- **Sector clears always count**: fast warden kills are no longer thrown out, a second tab can't end your run, and the launch bar says what opens the next sector.
- **Every model has its own price**: fancier guns, turrets and devices now cost a little more than the starter model and ask for a signature material (iridium for beams, xenite for railguns and mortars). The Workshop also shows each weapon's damage type.
- **Capture the beacons**: each sector's two beacons now start in raider hands. Kill the guards, hold through up to four waves and the beacon is yours: a place to extract, a friendly picket (green on your scopes) and +10% ore from the rocks around it, shown on the sector map. After 32 hours raiders may come back for one, and you beat them off alone.
- **Bigger hostiles**: the Ravager gunship and the Mauler siege cruiser now fly with patrols and lead the beacon waves.
- **Big ships feel big**: heavier hulls now fly with real mass. They build and shed speed slowly, swing into turns instead of snapping, barely bank, shrug off hits that rattle a scout, and rumble deeper. The camera also sits closer and higher, so a Bastion or Leviathan fills the screen with its deck running out ahead of you.
- **Proper asteroids**: the rocks were resculpted with craters, broken faces, ridges and gullies, a weathered stone surface and bigger crystal seams growing out of the crater floors. The giant ones finally look like mountains.
- **Materials always in view**: the hangar's top bar now shows everything in your hold under your coins and gems, on every page and every screen size.
- **Void Runner overhaul**: every player hull, turret, drone, hostile, warden, the station, beacons and wrecks were rebuilt with far more detail, hulls gained panel seams and weathering, explosions, engine plumes and shield hits were redone, the whole soundscape was resynthesised, and the flight HUD and debrief got a new look. The jump gate and the Free Trader now have their own models.
- **Closer chase camera**: your ship takes up more of the screen in flight.
- **Wingmen are real drones**: the Wingmen skill now calls in the same escort drones carriers fly, with the same flight brain, for as long as the skill lasts.
- **Two new things to dodge**: Maulers and Brood Carriers fire a slow storm rocket that turns badly and leaves a static storm where it bursts; Ravagers and Bulwarks paint a lance strike just ahead of you that detonates two seconds later.
- **Smarter missiles**: homing missiles lead their target, brake into the turn instead of orbiting slow or parked targets, and now steer for rocks too.
- **Fewer, heavier turrets**: hulls carry far fewer turret mounts (1 to 2 on small ships, 3 on the Kestrel and Aegis, 4 on the Bastion, 6 on the Leviathan) and every turret hits 50% harder. Gun platforms and capital hulls add a turret damage bonus on top (Kestrel and Aegis +15%, Bastion +25%, Leviathan +30%), shown in the ship's stats, with visibly bigger mounts. Turrets that no longer fit are back in your hangar.
- **Tougher rocks**: asteroids have 25% more health.
- **Mining Rig toned down**: +10% mining per level instead of +25%.
- **Hulls cost real materials**: shipyard material prices are about three times higher. Coin and gem prices are unchanged.
- **Polytown advisor**: the upgrades suggested under the builders chip are now chosen by an AI advisor that reads your town. It suggests again each time you take one.
- **Cheaper jewel mine**: the Jewel Mine needs 2 workers per level instead of 5, and the market now takes 3,400 jewels per gem instead of 3,600.
- **Polytown repainted**: every building, road, tree, cloud and patch of grass was redrawn in a warmer style. Buildings now fill their whole tile, so streets read as proper terraces, and houses come in different plaster and roof colours. A building changes its look at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20. Farms stay golden fields, lumber camps stay pine stands and quarries stay stone pits as they grow.
