---
title: Void Runner Refit
description: New ships, turrets and drones, a calmer hangar, and deep sectors that demand real gear.
date: 2026-09-19
---

- **Void Runner rebuilt**: new models for every ship, turret, drone, hostile and asteroid, plus new explosions, sounds, HUD and debrief. The camera sits closer.
- **Cleaner hangar**: every page was redesigned, Launch is always in the top bar and your materials show under your coins.
- **Capture the beacons**: clear a beacon's guards and hold through the waves to own it. You get a place to extract, a friendly picket and +10% ore nearby. Raiders may come back after 32 hours.
- **New hostiles**: Ravager gunships and Mauler siege cruisers fly with patrols and lead beacon waves. Watch for slow storm rockets and lance strikes painted ahead of you.
- **Deep sectors bite back**: from sector 3 hostiles hit far harder and wardens have more hull. Armour and shields give about two thirds more.
- **Sector clears always count**: fast warden kills count, and a second tab can't end your run.
- **Fewer, heavier turrets**: hulls carry 1 to 6 mounts and every turret hits 50% harder. The Kestrel, Aegis, Bastion and Leviathan add 15% to 30% on top. Turrets that no longer fit are back in your hangar.
- **Big ships feel big**: heavy hulls build speed slowly and swing into turns.
- **Wingmen and missiles**: Wingmen calls in real escort drones. Homing missiles lead their target and steer for rocks too.
- **Hull prices**: ships cost about three times the materials, and up to 80% more again for the biggest hulls. Coins and gems are unchanged.
- **Gear prices**: fancier models cost a little more and need a signature material. The Workshop shows each weapon's damage type.
- **Mining**: asteroids have 25% more health and the Mining Rig gives +10% per level instead of +25%.
- **Loadout**: hovering a socketed mod shows what it does. Level pins are grey and green.
- **Polytown repainted**: everything was redrawn in a warmer style. Buildings fill their tile and change look at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20.
- **Polytown advisor**: an AI advisor that reads your town now picks the suggested upgrades.
- **Cheaper jewel mine**: 2 workers per level instead of 5, and 3,400 jewels per gem instead of 3,600.
- **Leaderboard**: every pilot shows their power and level. Click one to see their ship, turrets and gear.
- **Hull refits**: lift any ship you own a tier at a time from the hangar. Each refit adds 30% hull and shield, 15% cargo and 15% turret damage, and costs a little less than a new hull of that tier. Slots and looks stay the same. Hover the refit to see the gains.
