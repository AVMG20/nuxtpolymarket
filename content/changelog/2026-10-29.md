---
title: Nitro Rush hold-to-drift
description: Nitro Rush drifts now last as long as you hold the drift button, and the wheel tightens or widens the line.
date: 2026-10-29
---

- **Nitro Rush**: hold Space (or the touch pedal) and steer to drift; the drift lasts until you let go, and steering away widens the line instead of ending it.
