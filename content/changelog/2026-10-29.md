title: Riftborn, smoother Dead Front and the Nitro Rush track builder
description: New game Riftborn, a horde-survival roguelike. Dead Front runs smoother, scrap glows at night, and Esc closes the workbench without pausing. Build your own Nitro Rush tracks with jumps, loopings and corkscrews, race your best lap's ghost, and see other players live.
date: 2026-10-29
---

- **Riftborn**: a new horde-survival roguelike with six classes, castable abilities, evolving weapons, elite affixes, five bosses and a skill tree.
- **Dead Front workbench**: Esc closes the workbench (or chat) and you play straight on, without the pause menu.
- **Dead Front performance**: fewer stutters on laptops, steadier server ticks and lighter updates, so zombies move smoother and closer to real time.
- **Dead Front scrap**: scrap glows faintly gold on the ground, so it's easy to spot at night.
- **Dead Front fixes**: holding F always starts a revive, headshots no longer ding twice, and clicks while typing in chat no longer shoot.
- **Dead Front squads**: squads now take up to 5 soldiers.
- **Nitro Rush**: hold Space (or the touch pedal) and steer to drift; the drift lasts until you let go, and steering away widens the line instead of ending it.
- **Nitro Rush**: a track builder under My Tracks: lay pre-built sections into your own loops and sprints, then drive them.
- **Nitro Rush**: stunt sections for the builder: jumps, loopings, corkscrews, banked bends and steep ramps.
- **Nitro Rush**: a ghost car replays your best lap so you can race it; toggle it with the ghost button.
- **Nitro Rush**: other players driving the same track now show up live as ghost cars with their name.
