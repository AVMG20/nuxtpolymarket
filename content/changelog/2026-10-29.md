---
title: Nitro Rush track builder, stunts and ghosts
description: Build your own Nitro Rush tracks with jumps, loopings and corkscrews, race your best lap's ghost, and see other players live.
date: 2026-10-29
---

- **Nitro Rush**: hold Space (or the touch pedal) and steer to drift; the drift lasts until you let go, and steering away widens the line instead of ending it.
- **Nitro Rush**: a track builder under My Tracks: lay pre-built sections into your own loops and sprints, then drive them.
- **Nitro Rush**: stunt sections for the builder: jumps, loopings, corkscrews, banked bends and steep ramps.
- **Nitro Rush**: a ghost car replays your best lap so you can race it; toggle it with the ghost button.
- **Nitro Rush**: other players driving the same track now show up live as ghost cars with their name.
