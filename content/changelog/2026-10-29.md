---
title: Riftborn and smoother Dead Front
description: New game Riftborn, a horde-survival roguelike. Dead Front runs smoother, scrap glows at night, and Esc closes the workbench without pausing.
date: 2026-10-29
---

- **Riftborn**: a new horde-survival roguelike with six classes, castable abilities, evolving weapons, elite affixes, five bosses and a skill tree.
- **Dead Front workbench**: Esc closes the workbench (or chat) and you play straight on, without the pause menu.
- **Dead Front performance**: fewer stutters on laptops, steadier server ticks and lighter updates, so zombies move smoother and closer to real time.
- **Dead Front scrap**: scrap glows faintly gold on the ground, so it's easy to spot at night.
- **Dead Front fixes**: holding F always starts a revive, headshots no longer ding twice, and clicks while typing in chat no longer shoot.
- **Dead Front squads**: squads now take up to 5 soldiers.
