---
title: Polytown monuments
description: Research is gone. Build five world wonders instead, stage by stage, for town-wide perks.
date: 2026-09-28
---

- **Polytown monuments**: research is replaced by the Great Pyramid, Colosseum, Lighthouse of Alexandria, Arc de Triomphe and Eiffel Tower, built in eight stages by their own crew, each stage adding a town-wide perk.
- **Your research carries over**: each branch became a monument, and placing it puts it up for free at the stage you had researched to.
