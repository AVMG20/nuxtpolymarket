## Pirate Raid

- **Paid for surviving**: coins tick up every second you stay afloat. Kills and chests no longer pay.
- **Letters of Marque**: a new Armory investment that raises all voyage pay, up to ×3.
- **Salvage upgrades**: ten upgrades that last the whole voyage, glowing by rarity.
- **New enemies and bosses**: Fire Ship, Harpooner, Mortar Barge, Tidecaller, the Kraken and the Phantom Admiral.
- **Rogue Wave**: a new ability that shoves ships and washes away enemy shot.
- Redrawn ships with visible guns, a bigger sea, new sound, and WASD and Space controls.

## Slots

- **All six slots** have a new cabinet look, bigger win celebrations, clearer paytables and better sound.
- **Same controls everywhere**: bet − / +, max bet, turbo, auto spin and Space to spin. Buying a bonus now asks you to confirm.
- **Xeno Slot** moves to outer space, and a UFO now collects the coins in its Hold & Win bonus.
- **Book of Shadows**: the rules now match the game. The free spins multiplier only applies to wild runs.
- **New slot: [Trash Panda Heist](/games/trashpanda)**. Raccoons rob the city across 1,024 ways, with sticky multiplier wilds in the free spins and a Dumpster Dive pick game.
- **Trash Panda Heist** and **Xeno Slot** now stamp each win in big numbers over the winning symbols.
- **Candy Madness** has a wider control bar, so big wins and balances fit.
- **Xeno Slot** has a slimmer, cleaner cabinet frame and background music without the humming drone. A big Hold & Win payout now goes straight into the win count-up instead of showing the total first.

- **Trash Panda Heist** has redrawn characters and loot, a slimmer cabinet, clearer bonus picks and a refreshed paytable. Bonus symbols now have bold frames and bigger labels, and a raccoon peeks out from under the Dumpster Dive lid.

- **Xeno Slot** has polished space artwork, clearer Wild and Portal symbols, a piloted UFO collector, a violet cabinet and a refreshed flight manual.
- Fixed the bright backdrop when opening the Trash Panda Heist info menu in dark mode.
- **Trash Panda Heist**: three or more Dumpsters anywhere now start the Dumpster Dive, so three on screen always pays off. Dumpsters are a little rarer to keep the game at 98% RTP.
- **Near-miss suspense** in Trash Panda Heist, Xeno Slot and Spiñata now teases one reel at a time, each slowing to a crawl before it stops, in normal and turbo speed. Before, the teased reels could all drop at once. In Fire in the Hole and Aether Gates the near-miss glow now follows the drop column by column.

## Polytown

- Recommended upgrades work again and update instantly as your town changes.

## Void Runner

- **Sovereign** is cheaper: 2.5b coins instead of 5b, and fewer warp cores, iridium, xenite and alloy. It also flies faster and turns quicker.
- The chase camera sits further back on big hulls, so the whole ship stays in view.
