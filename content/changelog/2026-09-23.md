## Pirate Raid overhaul

- **Paid for surviving**: coins now land every second you stay afloat, and the rate climbs as the voyage goes on. Kills and treasure chests no longer pay. Survive all six minutes for the completion bonus; sink early and you keep what you earned.
- **Letters of Marque**: a new Armory investment. Each of its ten levels raises all voyage pay by 20%, up to ×3, and pays for itself in about five voyages.
- **Salvage with rarities**: the old pile of timed power-ups is replaced by ten upgrades that last the whole voyage, from common to legendary. Crates glow in their rarity colour so you can tell what's worth the detour. Bosses always drop an epic or better.
- **New enemies**: the Fire Ship rams and explodes, the Harpooner tethers and slows you, the Mortar Barge shells marked spots from far away, and the Tidecaller shields the ships around it.
- **Two new bosses**: the Kraken surfaces under the fleet and slams its tentacles down, and the Phantom Admiral blinks across the sea, raises ghost escorts and fires spiralling volleys. They take turns with the Dreadnought.
- **Rogue Wave**: a new ability that rolls a wall of water toward the cursor, hitting and shoving every ship in its path and washing away enemy shot and mines.
- **Redrawn sea**: every ship is drawn fresh, with each of your guns visible on deck, aiming at what it's shooting at and tipped in its tier's colour. The sea is about a quarter bigger.
- **New sound** and a new look for every Pirate Raid page, including the Armory, the Log, the Almanac and the Legends board. You can now also steer with WASD and cast your ability with Space.

## Polytown suggestions work again

- **Recommended upgrades are back**: the builders popover no longer gets stuck on "Thinking…" or "No suggestions right now." Suggestions are now worked out instantly from your town and update as it changes.
- **Houses come first** when jobs outnumber residents, or are about to. After that come buildings that make a good you're running out of, then parks and baths when the town is unhappy, and a warehouse when goods are piling up at the cap.
- Upgrades that would drain a good you're already short of, or add jobs nobody can fill, drop down the list.
