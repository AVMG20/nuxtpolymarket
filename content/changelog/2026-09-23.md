## Pirate Raid

- **Paid for surviving**: coins tick up every second you stay afloat. Kills and chests no longer pay.
- **Letters of Marque**: a new Armory investment that raises all voyage pay, up to ×3.
- **Salvage upgrades**: ten upgrades that last the whole voyage, glowing by rarity.
- **New enemies and bosses**: Fire Ship, Harpooner, Mortar Barge, Tidecaller, the Kraken and the Phantom Admiral.
- **Rogue Wave**: a new ability that shoves ships and washes away enemy shot.
- Redrawn ships with visible guns, a bigger sea, new sound, and WASD and Space controls.

## Slots

- **All six slots** have a new cabinet look, bigger win celebrations, clearer paytables and better sound.
- **Same controls everywhere**: bet − / +, max bet, turbo, auto spin and Space to spin. Buying a bonus now asks you to confirm.
- **Xeno Slot** moves to outer space, and a UFO now collects the coins in its Hold & Win bonus.
- **Book of Shadows**: the rules now match the game. The free spins multiplier only applies to wild runs.

## Polytown

- Recommended upgrades work again and update instantly as your town changes.
