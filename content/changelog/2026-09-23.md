---
title: Polytown Suggestions Work Again
description: The builders popover recommends upgrades again, based on what your town needs.
date: 2026-09-23
---

- **Recommended upgrades are back**: the builders popover no longer gets stuck on "Thinking…" or "No suggestions right now." Suggestions are now worked out instantly from your town and update as it changes.
- **Houses come first** when jobs outnumber residents, or are about to. After that come buildings that make a good you're running out of, then parks and baths when the town is unhappy, and a warehouse when goods are piling up at the cap.
- Upgrades that would drain a good you're already short of, or add jobs nobody can fill, drop down the list.
