title: Nitro Rush arenas
description: Nitro Rush loops can be laid from fields alone, and fields get branch detours and required checkpoints.
date: 2026-11-18
---

- **Nitro Rush arenas**: build a loop from fields alone.
- **Nitro Rush fields**: branches can leave and rejoin a field anywhere on its edge, and roads run through the middle of fields.
- **Nitro Rush checkpoints**: mark checkpoints every lap must pass, and every line counts whichever way you drive through it.
- **Nitro Rush medals**: field medal times are tougher.
