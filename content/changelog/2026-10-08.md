---
title: Cleaner supply routes
description: Polytown supply routes are now a smooth line, and suppliers glow instead of wearing markers.
date: 2026-10-08
---

- **Polytown**: supply routes are one smooth line that turns from green to orange to red at the tiles where deliveries start losing goods, and suppliers glow in their route's colour.
- **Polytown**: a workshop takes its goods from one supplier when one can cover the order (an upgraded farm takes over from its partner), without changing how much arrives.
