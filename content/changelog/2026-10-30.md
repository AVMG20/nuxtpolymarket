---
title: Nitro Rush HUD and builder
description: A new racing HUD in Nitro Rush, and a track builder with a 3D preview and drag-to-reorder sections.
date: 2026-10-30
---

- **Nitro Rush**: a new racing HUD with a minimap of the track, an analog speedometer, and your lap time, best and last three laps in the top right.
- **Nitro Rush builder**: a 3D preview of your track, drag sections through the list to reorder them, and new half and quarter spirals; a half spiral climbs high enough to cross over another road.
- **Nitro Rush**: start, checkpoint and finish lines now have a low arch, so roads can cross over them, and the grandstand by the start is gone.
