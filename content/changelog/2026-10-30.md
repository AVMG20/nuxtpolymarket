---
title: Nitro Rush community tracks
description: Publish your Nitro Rush tracks for everyone to race, plus a new racing HUD and a 3D preview in the track builder.
date: 2026-10-30
---

- **Nitro Rush**: a new racing HUD with a minimap of the track, an analog speedometer, and your lap time, best and last three laps in the top right.
- **Nitro Rush builder**: a 3D preview of your track, drag sections through the list to reorder them, new half and quarter spirals that climb high enough to cross over another road, the start line can now sit up on a raised road, and the 50 m straight is now 60 m so courses line up on a 20 m grid.
- **Nitro Rush**: start, checkpoint and finish lines now have a low arch, so roads can cross over them, and the grandstand by the start is gone.
- **Nitro Rush**: the start lights sit centred on the arch, your car always shows in front of ghosts, and the ghost races your fastest lap ever, carrying on from the line on a loop and following you to any device.
- **Nitro Rush community tracks**: publish your own tracks for everyone to race on a shared leaderboard, browse them in the new Community tab, and race the track record's ghost; each kind of ghost can be switched on or off.
