- **Live Blackjack Release** — launched real-time multiplayer Blackjack featuring an authoritative table engine, WebSocket synchronization, chip ladders, and felt action controls.
- **Felt Table Rendering** — built a custom Pixi.js table renderer with animated shoe card draining, action turn markers, and player seat indicators.
- **Storm the House Minigame** — introduced the base defense minigame with upgrade progression, enemy waves, and dynamic audio effects.
