- **Sidebar**: build your own: star games on the home page, make folders, drag things around and right-click to manage.
- **Home page**: a fresh look with a picture for every game.
- **Colony**: a brand-new underground look, and spare bugs can be released straight from your inventory.
- **Xeno, Colony and Polytown**: planting, harvesting, placing, building, selling and more now show up the moment you click.
- **Xeno breeder**: Fill All, Clear, Start All and Collect All for every pod at once.
- **Leaderboard**: a roomier player card with their income over the last 7 days and where it came from.
- **AI Assistant**: the assistant and its wiki page have been retired.
