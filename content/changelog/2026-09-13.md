---
title: Polytown UI Revamp
description: A cleaner Polytown in light and dark, more goals, and a builder that suggests upgrades.
date: 2026-09-13
---

- **UI revamp** — every Polytown menu, window and tooltip rebuilt, with a proper light theme.
- **50 goals** — most are chains now, shown one step at a time.
- **Recommended upgrades** — hover the builders chip, click to start one.
- **Income breakdown** — hover the `/day` chip to see what each good earns.
- **Needs follow the population**, not the tier — and only residents past a need's threshold count.
- **Smithy** costs no tools to upgrade until level 5.
- **Notifications** — the bell in the top right lists finished builds, banked research and offers other mayors filled, including while you were away.
- **Market faces** — the order book shows each mayor's emblem.
- **Bulldozer removed** — select and press Delete instead.
- **Redesign mode** — the ruler button top right lifts the whole town into a tray so you can lay it out again. Nothing is saved until you confirm; roads left in the tray are removed, and new ones can be laid.
