---
title: Polytown Tier-3 Needs
description: Townsfolk no longer eat a new workshop's whole output the moment tier 3 unlocks.
date: 2026-09-13
---

- **Needs capped by output** — what your townsfolk ask for still grows with the population, but never past half of what your own workshops make of that good per tick. Tier 3 opens onto 80+ residents wanting 4 bread and 2 tools a tick, which a fresh level-1 bakery or smithy could never cover, so the stock sat at zero and the smithy could never bank the 25 tools its own upgrade needs. A new workshop now keeps its first loaves and tools, and the population takes over as the limit once it has grown past them. A good you have no workshop for is still asked for in full.
