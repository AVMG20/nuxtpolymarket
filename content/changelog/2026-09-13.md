---
title: Polytown Needs Follow the Population
description: Unlocking a tier no longer makes your townsfolk suddenly want more.
date: 2026-09-13
---

- **Needs no longer switch on with a tier** — what your townsfolk eat and use now depends on the population alone. Before, bread and tools were only wanted once the first tier-3 workshop stood, so finishing a bakery at 80+ residents instantly added 4 bread and 2 tools a tick of demand against a level-1 workshop that makes 1. A town of 80 now wants the same bread the day before it builds a bakery as the day after, the resource rail shows that deficit honestly, and nothing jumps on the way up. Going without a good you cannot make yet still costs no happiness; the moment you can make it, it does.
- **Needs popover** — lists every want, including goods the town cannot make yet, with how much an hour and which building makes it.
