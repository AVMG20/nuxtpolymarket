---
title: Storage and redesign drafts
description: Polytown buildings can be stored off the map, and a half-done redesign can be saved as a draft.
date: 2026-10-09
---

- **Polytown**: a building you are moving can be dropped back on its own spot, and the move hint shows that R turns it.
- **Polytown**: store buildings off the map and put them back from the new Storage tab in Build.
- **Polytown**: a redesign with pieces still in the tray saves as a draft you can finish later, and pieces can be sent to storage from the tray.
