---
title: Void Runner Pays What You Saw
description: The hold in the debrief is the hold you watched fill during the run.
date: 2026-09-22
---

- **The debrief matches the HUD**: Void Runner no longer re-checks your haul on the way home. The station used to trim it against hidden per-minute limits, a warp core allowance and the sector's ore list, so the debrief often showed less than the hold you had just filled. Every unit the cargo counter showed is now banked.
- **Relic and gear caches**: every cache you pick up counts, instead of a number worked out from how long the run lasted and which bosses died. The daily limits still apply.
- **Bounty XP**: a run's bounties pay in full whatever its length.
- The only check left is a sanity one: a report claiming far more than any hold can carry is refused instead of quietly corrected.
