---
title: Polytown Gets Its Colour Back
description: Taller houses with gardens and teal domes, and roof colour that tells you the level.
date: 2026-09-22
---

- **Houses have gardens again**: every house is now an L-shaped block with a walled front garden in the free corner: lawn, path, fence, a tree and flowers. Streets get shadows and green between the roofs instead of one flat carpet.
- **Roofs show the level**: every building's roof follows one ramp as it upgrades: terracotta at level 1, brick red at 5, wine at 10, steel blue at 15 and gold at 20. Nothing else about a plot's colour changes from tile to tile, so a golden street is a flex you can read from across the map.
- **Teal domes are back**: turret caps and domes are the town's teal on every level. Houses grow corner towers from level 10 and a central dome from level 15.
- **Taller town**: houses start at two storeys and reach six, and the mill, warehouse, foundry, factory, smithy, bathhouse, theatre and emporium all rise higher with each look.
- **More detail**: brighter plaster, darker roof edges, window frames, cornices, half-timbered facades, weathervanes and a clock cupola on big warehouses.
