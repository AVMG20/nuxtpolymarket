---
title: Polytown Gets Its Colour Back
description: Taller, brighter buildings, with roof colour that tells you the level.
date: 2026-09-22
---

- **Roofs show the level**: every building's roof and caps follow one ramp as it upgrades: orange at level 1, red at 5, teal at 10, blue at 15, plum at 20. Nothing else about a plot's colour changes from tile to tile, so a street reads as levels at a glance.
- **Taller town**: houses start at two storeys and reach six, and the mill, warehouse, foundry, factory, smithy, bathhouse, theatre and emporium all rise higher with each look.
- **More contrast and detail**: brighter plaster, darker roof edges, window frames, cornices, half-timbered facades, corner turrets and dormers from level 10, weathervanes, a clock cupola on big warehouses.
