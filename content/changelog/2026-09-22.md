---
title: Void Runner Pays What You Saw
description: The hold in the debrief is the hold you watched fill during the run.
date: 2026-09-22
---

- **The debrief matches the HUD**: Void Runner no longer re-checks your haul on the way home. The station used to trim it against hidden per-minute limits, a warp core allowance and the sector's ore list, so the debrief often showed less than the hold you had just filled. Every unit the cargo counter showed is now banked.
- **Relic and gear caches**: every cache you pick up counts, instead of a number worked out from how long the run lasted and which bosses died. The daily limits still apply.
- **Bounty XP**: a run's bounties pay in full whatever its length.
- The only check left is a sanity one: a report claiming far more than any hold can carry is refused instead of quietly corrected.

## SHAPEZZ overhaul

- **A new look**: glowing neon shapes, layered explosions, shattering enemies, smoke, sparks, screen shake and hit-stop on big kills. The arena lighting shifts as the mutations stack up, the floor sits on a perspective grid, and it renders sharp on high-resolution screens.
- **New sound**: every effect is now synthesized live instead of played from a file, with stereo panning and no clipping when the screen fills up. The music is unchanged.
- **New enemies**:
  - **Splitters** burst into fast shards when they die.
  - **Bombers** arm a fuse next to you. Kill one first and it blows up its friends instead.
  - **Snipers** paint you with a laser before firing.
  - **Wardens** halve the damage their neighbours take.
  - **Elite** versions of every shape carry a gold crown and four times the loot.
- **Four bosses** now take turns: **The Overseer**, **The Prism** (sweeping lasers and splitting shards), **The Hive Queen** (broods, homing missiles, falling rain) and **The Impossible Polygon** (charges, bullet spirals and floor slams you have to jump). Each gets angrier at two-thirds and one-third health. From wave 10 on Mayhem and Annihilation, two bosses arrive at once.
- **Random arenas**: every run gets its own layout of ledges, sometimes many and wide, sometimes a few narrow ones.
- **HUD**: a wave counter at the top right that warns when a boss is next, and the boss health bar at the top of the screen.
- **Rail Driver**: a new weapon. Its slug hits every shape on the line, and with Pinball Murder it bounces off the walls.
- **Scatter Array rework**: it now fires tight volleys of seeker missiles that curve onto shapes near your aim. Anyone who owned it keeps their rarity.
- **Two new mutations**: **Overcharge** (critical hits) and **Prism Lance** (a screen-piercing beam every few seconds).
- **Mutation changes**:
  - Pocket Singularity now runs on a timer, so every weapon gets the same black holes.
  - Hypervelocity actually adds damage.
  - Orbital guns swat enemy shots out of the air.
  - Panic Field slows shots much more.
  - Taking a hit now halves your combo instead of wiping it.
- **Balance**:
  - The first boss is now beatable on a fresh account.
  - The late game hits a hard wall around wave 8 to 10.
  - Higher difficulties now pay more than Surge for strong builds.
  - The strongest common mutations have been trimmed.
- Your own shots never get dropped when the screen is full of bullets.
- **The workshop has a new look**: level meters, stat previews and rarity bars.
