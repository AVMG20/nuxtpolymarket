---
title: Meadowbrawl
description: A new top-down melee roguelite — six classes with real combos, thirty waves, coins, pets, and boons that get gloriously out of hand.
date: 2026-09-02
---

- **Meadowbrawl** — a new arcade game. Angled bird's-eye hack-and-slash in a painterly autumn meadow. One run every two hours (or rush it with gems), and it pays out in coins.
- **Combo chains** — left click advances the chain; let the window lapse and it resets. Finishers hit harder and launch enemies. Dodging cancels the combo.
- **Six classes** — every weapon is a class with two abilities on Q and E. Knight (Shield Wall, Rallying Cry), Berserker (Bloodrage, Rending Throw), Lancer (Skewer Charge, Javelin Rain), Assassin (Smoke Bomb, Fan of Knives), Juggernaut (Iron Skin, Seismic Line), Reaper (Soul Harvest, Death Mark). Battle Rhythm shortens those cooldowns too.
- **Stunning is the goal** — an additive glow pass for sparks, embers, motes and auras; the meadow slides from noon into golden hour and a blood dusk as the waves climb, with fireflies gathering and god rays drifting; afterimages when you sprint or dash; every archetype dies its own way; elite kills hit a white flash and a letterboxed slow-motion beat.
- **Sound** — swings and abilities have their own voice per class, hits sit in a little room echo, wind and birdsong give way to crickets at dusk, the waterfall gets louder as you approach, and a battle drone with a quickening heartbeat thickens with the wave.
- **Six weapons** — Knight's Sword (3-hit, dash-through slash), Greataxe (2-hit, ground slam), Ash Spear (4-hit thrusts, 360° sweep), Twin Daggers (5-hit, blink behind), Warhammer (2-hit, Sky Fall leap), Reaper's Scythe (3-hit crescents that drag enemies in, Reaper's Whirl). Pick one at the start — the boon pool is boons only. Each has its own swing cadence and its own slash — spam clicking can't beat it.
- **Thirty waves** — chargers from wave 1, swarmers by 3, shield-bearers by 5, thornspitters by 7, an elite every fourth wave, elites in pairs from 16, three on the last. Veterans start showing up past wave 10. Ten is a run; twenty is a feat; thirty takes a god build and the hands to use it.
- **Boons** — whirlwind finishers, lifesteal, tremor strikes, chain lightning, +1 combo hit, wind cutters, oversized weapons, frostbite, wildfire, extra dodge charge, volatile corpses, faster specials, bull rush, crits, flow state, berserker, bramble skin, bloodlust, titan grip, rolling thunder, overcharge, executioner, echo strike, gravity well, death blossom, phoenix feather, adrenaline, reaper's toll, colossus, mirror edge, cleaving wind, spectral blades, meteor shower, singularity. They stack, and they combine.
- **Enemies path** — a navigation grid floods from you every tenth of a second; nothing hides behind a boulder any more.
- **Dodge is instant** — the roll fires the moment Space goes down, cancelling your swing and any hit-stop with it. Hold Space and you roll, then break into a sprint. A press during a committed special is queued rather than eaten, and you can attack out of the tail of a roll.
- **Coins** — every foe drops a share of its wave's bounty; walk over the pile to bank it. The run pays out on death or victory, at your account's coin multiplier, with a bonus for clearing all thirty. The server only ever credits what the waves you provably cleared could have dropped.
- **Saved per wave** — every wave boundary writes a checkpoint, boons on offer included. Close the tab mid-run and the meadow offers to pick it back up, or to abandon it and collect what it had banked.
- **Stun meter** — bosses no longer get stun-locked by finishers. Every hit builds a meter (heavy hits, crits and finishers build it fastest); a full meter breaks the enemy open for a few vulnerable seconds, then the meter locks while it recovers.
- **Two new bosses** — the Briar Matriarch (thorn volleys, root snares, a brood of Spriglings) on waves 10 and 22, and the Hollow Knight (a three-hit combo, a parry that punishes impatience, a shadow step) on 14 and 26. Twenty elites in a full run.
- **Weapon feats** — you start with the Sword. Each other class is earned with the one before it: slay the Bog Ogre with the Sword, the Warlord with the Greataxe, the Matriarch with the Spear, the second Ogre with the Daggers, the Hollow Knight with the Warhammer.
- **Prosperity** — the one permanent upgrade, ten levels of +20% coin value. Nothing you buy makes the waves easier; that is the pets' job.
- **Pets** — adopt and level the Ember Fox (Flame Dash, Cinder Howl), the Moss Tortoise (Shell Ward, Mending Bloom) or the Moonlit Owl (Gust, Lucky Feather). Ten levels each: a passive that grows, one ability at three, a second at six, doubled potency at ten. Waves are a touch bigger to match.
- **Fourth batch of boons** — Magpie, Meadow's Mercy, Rolling Kick, Thunder Step, Kill Frenzy, Chronoshear, and two legendaries that show up once a run has legs: Avatar of the Meadow (every sixth swing is a field-wide sweep) and Stormcaller (lightning falls while your combo is live). Bloodthirst heals 4% instead of 6%.
- **Balance** — waves past ten scale harder in both health and damage, veterans arrive a wave earlier and more often. Ten to fifteen is a real fight, twenty to twenty-five is brutal, thirty wants the right legend and the hands to use it.
- **Feel** — hit-stop, damage-scaled shake, floating numbers, sparks and blood that stain the grass, knockback and stagger, swing trails, and a ground telegraph on every enemy attack.
