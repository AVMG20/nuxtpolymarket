---
title: Meadowbrawl
description: A new top-down melee roguelite — four weapons with real combos, twenty waves, and boons that get gloriously out of hand.
date: 2026-09-02
---

- **Meadowbrawl** — a new arcade game. Angled bird's-eye hack-and-slash in a painterly autumn meadow, all in the browser, no stakes, no save.
- **Combo chains** — left click advances the chain; let the window lapse and it resets. Finishers hit harder and launch enemies. Dodging cancels the combo.
- **Six weapons** — Knight's Sword (3-hit, dash-through slash), Greataxe (2-hit, ground slam), Ash Spear (4-hit thrusts, 360° sweep), Twin Daggers (5-hit, blink behind), Warhammer (2-hit, Sky Fall leap), Reaper's Scythe (3-hit crescents that drag enemies in, Reaper's Whirl). Pick one at the start — the boon pool is boons only. Each has its own swing cadence and its own slash — spam clicking can't beat it.
- **Thirty waves** — chargers from wave 1, swarmers by 3, shield-bearers by 5, thornspitters by 7, an elite every fourth wave, elites in pairs from 16, three on the last. Veterans start showing up past wave 10. Ten is a run; twenty is a feat; thirty takes a god build and the hands to use it.
- **Boons** — whirlwind finishers, lifesteal, tremor strikes, chain lightning, +1 combo hit, wind cutters, oversized weapons, frostbite, wildfire, extra dodge charge, volatile corpses, faster specials, bull rush, crits, flow state, berserker, bramble skin, bloodlust, titan grip, rolling thunder, overcharge, executioner, echo strike, gravity well, death blossom, phoenix feather, adrenaline, reaper's toll, colossus, mirror edge, cleaving wind, spectral blades, meteor shower, singularity. They stack, and they combine.
- **Enemies path** — a navigation grid floods from you every tenth of a second; nothing hides behind a boulder any more.
- **Feel** — hit-stop, damage-scaled shake, floating numbers, sparks and blood that stain the grass, knockback and stagger, swing trails, and a ground telegraph on every enemy attack.
