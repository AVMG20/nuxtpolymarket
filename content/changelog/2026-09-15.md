---
title: Hack Ops Auto-Deploy
description: Running ops can redeploy themselves on collect.
date: 2026-09-15
---

- **Auto-deploy for Hack Ops** — every running op now has an Auto-deploy switch. When it's on, collecting the op immediately sends the same squad back out on the same operation, so a grind loop is one click per cycle. The briefing screen has the same switch so a squad can start looping from its first deploy, and the flag carries over to each redeploy. If the redeploy can't happen (an agent moved to storage, or gear changed and the success chance dropped too low) you still get your payout and a toast explains why.
