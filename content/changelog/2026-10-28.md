---
title: Dead Front salvage rework and Nitro Rush sprints
description: Dead Front's zombies each have a chance to drop scrap, the bench is repriced to match, and the workbench is easier to use. Three new Nitro Rush tracks, with ramps, spirals and diagonals, a track menu, a sharper rear end for the car, and checkpoints that split each lap.
date: 2026-10-28
---

- **Dead Front salvage**: scrap is the only bench currency, every zombie can drop it, squads earn as much per player as solo, and the bench and weapon upgrades are repriced to match.
- **Dead Front workbench**: press F to open a proper shop with an icon and price for every item, pick with WASD, the arrows or the wheel, hold F to craft and press Esc to close.
- **Dead Front scopes**: telescopic sights now give a proper full-screen scope view, and the Winchester's iron sights no longer glitch while aiming.
- **Dead Front weapon plate**: your weapon's rarity now shows as a clear coloured label next to its name.
- **Maxim Silencer**: kills with it drop scrap 10% more often.
- **TCG trades**: start an offer from any card in your collection with the new Trade button, and search both sides of an offer by name or number.
- **Polytown**: its styling no longer leaks into other games after a visit, which jumbled the Dead Front workbench.
- **Nitro Rush**: two new sprints: the S-Bend Sprint, two long straights and an S, and the Zigzag Sprint, which cuts up the diagonals.
- **Nitro Rush**: roads can now climb: the new Twin Helix circuit runs up a ramp and through two stacked spirals, one each way.
- **Nitro Rush**: the car has a new rear end with a random Polynux ad on its plate, and a checkpoint on the Oval's back straight splits each lap in two.
- **Nitro Rush**: a new title screen, and DRIVE now opens a track and car menu with Circuits and Sprints, a spinning preview, the record and your best for every track.
- **Nitro Rush**: a new neon-tube font, the controls now list R to restart, and every run starts with a full nitro tank.
- **Nitro Rush**: letting go of the wheel mid-drift now holds the slide, so a whole spiral can be drifted.
