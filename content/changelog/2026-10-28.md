---
title: Nitro Rush gets sprints, spirals and diagonals
description: Three new Nitro Rush tracks, with ramps, spirals and diagonals, a track menu, a sharper rear end for the car, and checkpoints that split each lap.
date: 2026-10-28
---

- **Nitro Rush**: two new sprints: the S-Bend Sprint, two long straights and an S, and the Zigzag Sprint, which cuts up the diagonals.
- **Nitro Rush**: roads can now climb: the new Twin Helix circuit runs up a ramp and through two stacked spirals, one each way.
- **Nitro Rush**: the car has a new rear end with a random Polynux ad on its plate, and a checkpoint on the Oval's back straight splits each lap in two.
- **Nitro Rush**: a new title screen, and DRIVE now opens a track and car menu with Circuits and Sprints, a spinning preview, the record and your best for every track.
- **Nitro Rush**: a new neon-tube font, the controls now list R to restart, and every run starts with a full nitro tank.
- **Nitro Rush**: letting go of the wheel mid-drift now holds the slide, so a whole spiral can be drifted.
