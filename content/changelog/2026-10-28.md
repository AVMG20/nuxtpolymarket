title: Dead Front salvage rework

- **Dead Front salvage**: every zombie has a chance to drop scrap, so bigger hordes pay more, and the workbench and upgrades are repriced to match.
- **Dead Front workbench**: pick with the mouse wheel or ↑/↓ and hold F to craft.
- **Maxim Silencer**: kills with it drop scrap 10% more often.
