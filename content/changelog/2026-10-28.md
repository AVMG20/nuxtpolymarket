---
title: Dead Front salvage rework
description: Dead Front's zombies each have a chance to drop scrap, the bench is repriced to match, and the workbench is easier to use.
date: 2026-10-28
---

- **Dead Front salvage**: scrap is the only bench currency, every zombie can drop it, squads earn as much per player as solo, and the bench and weapon upgrades are repriced to match.
- **Dead Front workbench**: press F to open a proper shop with an icon and price for every item, pick with the wheel or arrows, hold F to craft and press Esc to close.
- **Dead Front weapon plate**: your weapon's rarity now shows as a clear coloured label next to its name.
- **Maxim Silencer**: kills with it drop scrap 10% more often.
