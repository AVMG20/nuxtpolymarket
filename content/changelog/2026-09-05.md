---
title: Meadowbrawl boons
description: The boon suite rebuilt — three elemental schools, spectral allies, pacts with a catch, cards you can read at a glance, and a roller that deals the tier it says it deals.
date: 2026-09-05
---

- **Tier-first roller** — a card's odds used to depend on how many cards its tier held, so rares turned up more than commons and a legendary almost never. Now the tier is rolled first, then the card. Legends enter at wave 6 as a rare roll, no run is owed one, and two is the most you will ever hold.
- **Three schools** — Fire, Ice and Shock. Every elemental boon carries its school on the card, and stacking a school pays off: five Wildfire stacks make burning foes take more from everything; three Frostbite stacks start a chill meter that freezes foes solid; Static Charge marks a target so your hits crit against it, and a crit on a charged body arcs to its neighbour. Fire from any source ignites when you hold Wildfire; ice from any source chills when you hold Frostbite.
- **New boons** — Rippling Blows (every hit spills onto the crowd), Spectral Vanguard (a ghost warrior at your side, then an archer, then another warrior), Static Charge, Shatter (frozen kills burst into frost), Opening Strike, Thick Hide, Sure Footing, Second Helping, Fortune (a fourth card on every offer), Last Stand, and three legends: Conflagration (burning foes light their neighbours), Deep Winter (a frost wave every ten seconds) and Eclipse (every 25th hit stops time).
- **Pacts** — Glass Cannon, Gambler's Edge and Blood Pact trade something away for something bigger. The catch is printed in red.
- **Merged and reworked** — Thunder Step folded into Rolling Thunder (stacks add the clap at the start), Kill Frenzy folded into Bloodlust (kills now stack speed and damage together, with flames that climb), Tremor Strikes became Rippling Blows, Reaper's Toll became Cold Snap, Mirror Edge retired. Keen Edge is 10% a stack, Colossus trades its damage for knockback and stun build, Meadow's Mercy heals a share of your health instead of a flat point, Chain Lightning is a chance rather than a certainty, and Mending is only dealt when you are actually hurt.
- **Cards** — one line of text, a badge for what the boon *is* (a buff, an effect, a pact), a badge for its school, stack pips instead of a counter, and a legendary that shimmers when it lands.
- **Seen, not read** — Berserker beats red, Bloodlust burns brighter with every stack, Flow State ripples while the chain is live, Static crawls over charged bodies, frost creeps across the ground as the chill meter fills, and a school you have invested in shows on you.
