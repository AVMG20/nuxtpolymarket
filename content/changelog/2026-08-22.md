---
title: Call of Xeno Perks and Power
description: Two new weapons and perks, Death Machine and Carpenter drops, Pack-a-Punch quirks, a wandering mystery box, an upstairs window — and a window escape fix.
date: 2026-08-22
---

- **Window leak fixed** — no more jumping out through boarded windows.
- **Mosin-Nagant** — new bolt-action in the box. One slow, brutal shot.
- **M1 Bazooka** — new launcher in the box, now at 1,000 damage. Wide blast, one shell at a time.
- **Deadshot** — headshots hit for 2x. Lives upstairs in Overwatch.
- **PhD Flopper** — your blasts never hurt you, +35% blast radius. In the Mess Hall, between Barracks and Garage.
- Still a **4 perk belt** — six machines now, choose carefully.
- **Death Machine drop** — 20s belt-fed minigun, no reload, wide cone.
- **Carpenter drop** — re-boards every window on the map, pays 200.
- **Pack-a-Punch quirks** — Bazooka splits into cluster warheads; Mosin headshots hit 2.5x with no falloff.
- **The mystery box moves** — starts at a random anchor (Atrium or Garage by Speed Cola), 15% chance it walks between rounds.
- **Upstairs window** — zombies can now scale into Overwatch.
- **Directional audio** — groans and breaches pan to the side they come from.
- **After-action stats** — accuracy and points/min on the death screen.
- **Flat points** — every enemy pays the same, shambler to Brute.
