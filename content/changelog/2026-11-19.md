title: Field Obstacles and Cloverfield
description: Two new Nitro Rush field courses, and required checkpoints on several branches off one field.
date: 2026-11-19

- **Field Obstacles**: new Nitro Rush sprint through a maze, a pillar X and a boost field.
- **Cloverfield**: new Nitro Rush circuit with three loops off one field, in any order.
- **Nitro Rush builder**: several branches off one field can now each hold a required checkpoint.
- **Nitro Rush medals**: the bot now drives between pillars, so fields full of pillars get medals.
