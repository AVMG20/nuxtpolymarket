---
title: Hero Quest Arena opponents, Polytown selection, gems in the audit and Nitro Rush track votes
description: Players without a defence show up in the Arena, an empty refresh is no longer charged, selected Polytown buildings light up blue, and the audit tracks gems. Vote on community tracks, sort by popular, and publish with your own lap.
date: 2026-11-02
---

- **Hero Quest Arena**: players who never set a defence now show up as opponents with their current party, and a refresh that can't find anyone new is no longer used up or charged.
- **Polytown**: selected buildings turn into a blue ghost instead of getting a ring on the ground, so they're easy to spot.
- **Audit**: a Coins / Gems switch shows gems too, starting today, and Hero Quest now shows its top players under Idle.
- **Bank**: refunds from cancelled offers, outbid TCG bids and crashed table rounds are no longer garnished.
- **Nitro Rush community tracks**: vote tracks up or down and sort by Newest or Top.
- **Nitro Rush publishing**: drive a lap before publishing; your time and ghost start the leaderboard.
- **Nitro Rush track menu**: slimmer cards, a top 5 for the selected track, and a crown on tracks where you hold the record.
- **Nitro Rush track builder**: sections now sit in a bar under the map, each with a small 3D preview, and there's a new 10 m straight.
- **Nitro Rush banks**: roll onto a bank with its own section, and every straight and corner stays banked, low edge on the ground, until you roll back to flat.
- **Nitro Rush slopes**: the track builder has real slopes that carry through straights and corners, more bend sizes, and a new BUILD mode that shows a ghost of the next piece.
- **Nitro Rush My Tracks**: delete a track right from its card.
