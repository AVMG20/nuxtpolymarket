---
title: Nitro Rush track votes
description: Vote on community tracks, sort by popular, and publish with your own lap.
date: 2026-11-02
---

- **Nitro Rush community tracks**: vote tracks up or down and sort by Newest or Top.
- **Nitro Rush publishing**: drive a lap before publishing; your time and ghost start the leaderboard.
- **Nitro Rush track menu**: slimmer cards, a top 5 for the selected track, and a crown on tracks where you hold the record.
