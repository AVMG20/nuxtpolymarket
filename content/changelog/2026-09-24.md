## New Game: Holdfast

- **[Holdfast](/holdfast)** is a new real-time castle defence game under Active Games. Raiders attack your keep in waves, and every building you own can be knocked down.
- Earn gold and wood, then build walls, gates, watchtowers and more. A Stonemason turns wood into stone for sturdier walls, and a Crystal Mine grows the crystals that pay for upgrades.
- Every extra building of the same kind costs more, so upgrading what you have is often the better deal.
- Drop packs of warriors and archers from the toolbar: rings and ghost soldiers show where they will stand. Archers dropped on a wall stand on top of it and shoot further.
- Packs fight on their own. Select one and right-click to march it somewhere else.
- Three maps: **Frontier** (attacks from the north only), **Borderlands** (from three sides) and **Heartland** (from every side).
- There is no pause, only 2× speed. At 20:00 the Bloodmoon rises and raiders grow stronger by the minute. Survive until 25:00 to win, and climb the survival leaderboard for each map.

## New Game: PolyMasters

- **[PolyMasters](/games/polymasters)** is a new crash game under Slots. Your plane takes off from an aircraft carrier, collects adders and multipliers, and dodges rockets that halve your winnings. Land on the island to cash out, or splash into the sea and lose the round.
- Grab boosters on the way: Nitro deflects rockets, the Laser Gun shoots them down, the Magnet pulls you toward the island, and the Life Buoy saves you from one splash.
- **Safe Landing** costs 50× your bet per flight and always lands on the island. The button shows the exact price for your current bet.
- Type any bet straight into the bet field, including shorthand like `2.5k` or `1m`, or pick a preset.
- Four flight speeds you can switch mid-flight, autoplay, and a fullscreen button. RTP is about 97.8% and the max win is 1,000×.

## New Slot: Ember Portals

- **[Ember Portals](/games/emberportals)** is a new 7×7 slot under Slots. Five or more matching symbols touching sideways or up and down pay, then burn away and new symbols tumble in.
- Every win without a portal opens a fire portal wild inside it. A portal that wins again gains +1 to its multiplier and jumps to a new spot, and portals in the same win merge and multiply: ×5 and ×3 become ×15.
- Three or more scatters give 10 to 18 free spins. Portals stay open for the whole round and keep growing.
- Free spins land about once every 150 spins. **Extra Chance** costs 1.25× your bet and brings that to about once every 113 spins. **Buy Free Spins** costs 100× your bet.
- RTP is about 97.8% on a normal spin or with Extra Chance, and 97.5% on a buy. Portals can grow up to ×2,500 and the max win is 10,000×.

## Aether Gates

- Detailed, high-resolution gems and golden relics, a new gate emblem and a calm celestial backdrop.
- A slimmer reel frame, clearer bonus cards and multiplier meter, and a compact control bar centered beneath the reels.
- The animated scatter portals are still here, with the same game rules and payouts.
- Long autoplay and turbo runs on Aether Gates no longer sign you out.

## Candy Madness

- A dark-first candy-land cabinet with a brighter marquee, glowing scenery, richer bonus cards and a bigger reel board.
- Sharper candy artwork, a compact control deck, and clearer bonus features and win amounts.
- Matching dark candy-land styling for the info, paytable and autoplay dialogs, with equal-height balance, bet and win displays.

## Leaderboard

- A clearer leaderboard: it now spells out how players are ranked (prestige, then upgrades, then net worth) and shows every player's net worth.
- A podium for the top three, plus a "Your rank" card that shows how far you are behind the next player and jumps to your row.
- Each player's upgrades now show as a colored bar split by Colony, Xeno, Polytown and Void Runner, and the columns have plain labels instead of bare icons.
- Works on phones without scrolling sideways, and player details now group stats by game.

## Slots

- **No more surprise sign-outs**: long autoplay and turbo runs on Aether Gates, Book of Shadows, Fire in the Hole and Trash Panda no longer log you out.
- Leaving a slot mid-spin now shows your correct balance straight away.
