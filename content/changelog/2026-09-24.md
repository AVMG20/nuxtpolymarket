title: Fresh Looks for Aether Gates and Candy Madness
description: Refreshed artwork and cleaner controls for Aether Gates and Candy Madness.

## Aether Gates

- Detailed, high-resolution gems and golden relics, a new gate emblem and a calm celestial backdrop.
- A slimmer reel frame, clearer bonus cards and multiplier meter, and a compact control bar centered beneath the reels.
- The animated scatter portals are still here, with the same game rules and payouts.

## Candy Madness

- A dark-first candy-land cabinet with a brighter marquee, glowing scenery, richer bonus cards and a bigger reel board.
- Sharper candy artwork, a compact control deck, and clearer bonus features and win amounts.
- Matching dark candy-land styling for the info, paytable and autoplay dialogs, with equal-height balance, bet and win displays.

## Leaderboard

- A clearer leaderboard: it now spells out how players are ranked (prestige, then upgrades, then net worth) and shows every player's net worth.
- A podium for the top three, plus a "Your rank" card that shows how far you are behind the next player and jumps to your row.
- Each player's upgrades now show as a colored bar split by Colony, Xeno, Polytown and Void Runner, and the columns have plain labels instead of bare icons.
- Works on phones without scrolling sideways, and player details now group stats by game.
